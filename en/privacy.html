Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

In short

Sleevoo has no accounts and no server of ours. Everything you write stays on your iPhone. The app collects no data, we don't track you, and we sell nothing to anyone. The only exception is the optional waitlist on this website, described below.

What stays on your iPhone

Trips, bookings, attached documents, photos, notes, expenses, checklists and travellers are stored on your device only. They never pass through us: we have no server they could pass through. Text recognised in the documents you attach is read on the phone and stays there, so you can search inside your own tickets.

What leaves the phone, and to whom

Five things, and only when you make them happen. None of them says who you are.

Four go to Apple:

  • Place and address search (Apple Maps): when you type in a "Place" or "Address" field, what you are typing is sent to Apple to suggest results.
  • Maps: the map imagery comes from Apple while you look at it.
  • Weather (Apple Weather / WeatherKit): we send Apple the coordinates of your trip's destination to get the forecast.
  • Dictation: if you add a booking by speaking, the transcription is done by Apple's speech recognition, which may process the audio on Apple's own servers. It only happens while you are recording, and we say so there too, under the button.

One goes to Google, and it is the only thing that leaves towards anyone other than Apple:

  • Short Google Maps links: if you paste a link like "maps.app.goo.gl/…", the coordinates are not inside it — it is only a redirect. To find out where it leads, Sleevoo asks Google to open it. That link is sent and nothing else, only at the moment you paste it, and only if it is that kind of link. An already complete link sends nothing. We say so in the sheet where you paste it, too.

What NEVER leaves: your trips, bookings, notes, expenses, documents, attachments, photos, traveller names. There is no server of ours to send them to, and there is no account. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and the app contains no third-party libraries.

In none of the five cases do we send your name, your email or an identifier: Apple receives a piece of text, a pair of coordinates or audio; Google receives a link that you pasted. How they handle those is described in their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Camera: only if you choose "Scan" to photograph a ticket or a confirmation.
  • Calendar: to add a booking to your Calendar we ask for write-only access, which shows us nothing of what is already there. We ask for full access only if you tap "Import from Calendar", because that means reading your events.
  • Notifications: only for the reminders you switch on yourself.
  • Location: only if you tap the location button on the trip map, to show you where you are. We don't send it to ourselves and we don't store it; while you look at the map, Apple serves the imagery for the area on screen.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only if you add a booking by dictating it. The transcription is done by Apple's speech recognition, which may process the audio on Apple's own servers.
  • Photos: to pick an image we use the system picker, which hands us only the file you chose. Sleevoo has no access to your photo library.

Purchases

Sleevoo Full is bought through the App Store: Apple handles the payment entirely. We never see or keep any payment data. On the free plan you can keep 3 trips, with 6 bookings and 10 expenses each; with Full there is no limit at all.

Waitlist (website only)

If you join the waitlist on sleevoo.app we collect your email address, the date you signed up and, if present, the link you came from (for example a campaign tag such as "tiktok"). We use it only to email you when Sleevoo launches, and we process it based on your consent, which you give by ticking the box and can withdraw at any time. It is stored in a Google Sheet managed by Sleevoo, and the confirmation email is sent through Google, which acts as our service provider. We do not sell or share it. You can ask us to delete your email at any time at support@sleevoo.app. This applies only to the website: the app itself still collects nothing.

Children

Sleevoo is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deletion

You can delete a trip, a booking or a document at any time. Uninstalling the app removes everything you saved from the phone: no copy remains with us, because we never had one. If your phone's backup is on, a copy stays in your backup, as with any app, and you manage it yourself in Settings → iCloud.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears here with a new date.
