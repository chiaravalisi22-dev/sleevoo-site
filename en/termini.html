What Sleevoo is

An iPhone app that keeps a trip's bookings, documents and expenses together. It works without an account and without a connection, except for place suggestions, the map and the weather.

The free plan and Sleevoo Full

Free, you can keep 3 trips, with 6 bookings and 10 expenses each. Attachments, travellers and checklist lines are never counted. Sleevoo Full is bought once through the App Store, removes every limit, and stays yours on all devices signed in to your Apple ID. It is not a subscription and it does not renew.
