Support

Sleevoo is made by one person. Write to support@sleevoo.app: I answer, usually within two or three days.

If you're writing about a problem, tell me your iPhone model and iOS version — it saves two messages.

Frequently asked questions

How many trips can I create?

Free, you can keep 3 trips, with 6 bookings and 10 expenses each. Attachments, travellers and checklists are never counted. With Sleevoo Full, nothing is counted.

Is Sleevoo Full a subscription?

No — you pay once and it stays yours forever, on every device signed in to your Apple ID.

I changed phone: how do I get my purchase back?

Open Sleevoo, go to Profile and tap "Restore purchases". You need the same Apple ID you bought with.

Is my data safe?

Trips, documents and photos stay on your iPhone: there is no account and no server of ours. Only place searches and the weather leave the phone, and they go to Apple without saying who you are. The backup is your phone's backup: if you uninstall without one, nobody else has your data.

Do I get a reminder before each booking?

Yes, if you turn it on for that booking: you choose how long before.

Sleevoo is an iPhone app. No account, no server of ours.
